# TICKET-35 PART A Root-Cause Diagnosis

1. **Ghost ball without players (3D)**: Previously `PickleballCourt3D.tsx` ticked `stepPickleballRally` unconditionally without checking whether 4 agents were actually positioned in `court_nw..court_se` or whether the match was in `serve`/`rally` state. Moving match state into a pure reducer (`pickleballSimulation.ts`) and gating ball visibility/motion on `status === 'serve' || status === 'rally'` with `2v2` active players eliminates ghost balls permanently.
2. **2D and 3D are two separate implementations**: `OfficeCanvas2D.tsx` and `OfficeCanvas3D.tsx`/`OfficeFurniture.tsx`/`ProceduralAgent3D.tsx` each independently iterate `agents`, compute poses, and draw custom geometry. Introducing `src/lib/office/sceneModel.ts` (`buildSceneModel`) as the single pure frame builder and `src/lib/office/spriteBaker.ts` to bake 3D models into cached 2D atlases guarantees 100% 2D/3D parity.
3. **Clicking `Pickleball Court` pill summons all 31 employees**: `handleGlobalModeChange` originally mapped all `agents` onto `getAgentTargetPosition(ws, newMode)`. Guarding camera-only tabs/pills so they only update the camera focus target prevents any agent state mutation.
4. **No explicit passageway to the court**: While `pathfinding.ts` allowed `gz < -23`, `validateMap.ts` did not validate a >= 2-tile-wide corridor-to-court passage, threshold, or bench/basket reachability. Extending `CampusMap` + `validateMap.ts` fixes this.
5. **Players do not visibly move, shuffle, and swing at hit time**: `ProceduralAgent3D.tsx` used a generic sine wave on `rightArmRef` decoupled from `pickleballSimulation.ts`'s `flightProgress`. Driving player intercept offsets, facing, and `forehand`/`backhand`/`serve` swing keyframes directly from `buildSceneModel` synchronizes paddle contact with ball arrival within 1 frame.
6. **3D zoom/orbit locked**: `CameraController.tsx` previously overwrote `controls.target` and `orthoCam.zoom` inside `useFrame` on every frame. Using a one-shot `isTweeningRef` that cancels immediately on `OrbitControls` `onStart` (wheel/drag/pinch) and wiring `+`/`-`/`Recenter`/`Fit all` to `orthoCam.zoom` + `updateProjectionMatrix()` unlocks smooth 3D camera control.
7. **Waiting players must sit on sideline benches**: Adding capacity-8 (`2 benches x 4 seats`) queue state (`office_pickleball_queue`), queue badges (`Next`, `2nd`), orange foot ring, and post-game winner-stays rotation ensures waiting agents sit cleanly on benches rather than crowding the court.
